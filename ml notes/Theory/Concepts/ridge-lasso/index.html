How to study this workspace for long-term retention

Reading formulas creates fluency strength (it feels familiar while you look at it), but interviews test storage strength (recalling and deriving it cold on a blank whiteboard). Each lesson below is intentionally short, focuses on one mental model, pairs every step with its why, and closes with an immediate retrieval quiz and whiteboard drill.

Recommended cadence

Do Lessons 1–2 today, wait 24 hours, then do Lessons 3–4. Use Lesson 5 (Interleaved Interview Drills) 2–3 days later without opening the cheat sheet first.
